The studio

A small team that covers the whole pipeline, so your film, your stills and your launch graphics all come from one place and look like they belong together.

Trizar started as a 3D animation studio: modelling products, lighting them and turning them into launch films. Brands kept asking us for the pieces around the film too: the reel cut, the poster, the event backdrop, the social posts. So we built the team to make all of it.

Today we work across 3D animation and rendering, CGI and VFX, architectural visualisation, motion graphics and video editing, AI video, branding, print and event design. Every project is planned, made and checked in-house, so nothing gets lost between agencies.

We work with brands, agencies and event teams, on anything from one product render to a full launch campaign. Tell us the deadline and where the work will be seen, and we will plan around it.
